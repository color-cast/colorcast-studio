What we make and fix

Our services

Bring a design, a rough idea or a broken board. We tell you what is possible and what it costs before we start.

DTF Printing

Full-colour designs printed on film and heat pressed onto garments, with sharp detail and a soft feel.

T-shirts · hoodies · bags

Sublimation Printing

Bright, permanent prints on polyester and coated items. The colour becomes part of the surface and does not peel.

Mugs · plates · tiles · jerseys

Ari Work & Embroidery

Ari (maggam) work for blouses and ethnic wear, and machine embroidery for logos and names. We sew a test before the full run.

Blouses · ethnic wear · kurtis · T-shirts · shirts · uniforms

Social Media Management

We design posts and reels, write captions and keep your business page active, so customers see your work regularly.

Posts · reels · captions

3D Printing

Custom parts, prototypes and gifts printed from your 3D file or from a sketch we model for you.

PLA · PETG · prototypes · gifts

Electronics Repair & Custom Product Making

Diagnosis and repair for boards and devices. We also build custom products around your idea with ESP32, Arduino and 3D printed parts.

Fault finding · board repair · ESP32 / Arduino

Website Development

Websites and business tools for local shops and institutions: service pages, catalogues, billing and management apps that work on a phone.

Websites · Firebase apps · billing tools

About ColorCast

Made by people who build their own machines
