What we make and fix

Seven services, one studio

Bring a design, a rough idea or a broken board. We tell you what is possible and what it costs before we start.

DTF printing

Full-colour designs printed on film and heat pressed onto garments. Sharp detail, soft feel, no minimum quantity.

T-shirts · hoodies · bags · cotton, polyester, blends

Sublimation

Bright, permanent prints on polyester and coated items. The colour becomes part of the surface and does not peel.

Mugs · plates · tiles · sports jerseys

Poster printing

Posters, photo prints and display graphics for rooms, shops and events, in the size you need.

A4 · A3 · A2 · A1 · A0 · custom sizes

Embroidery

Machine embroidery for logos, names and patterns. We digitize your artwork into stitches and sew a test before the full run.

Caps · uniforms · shirts · blouses · ari (maggam) work

3D printing

Custom parts, prototypes, gifts and display pieces printed from your 3D file or from a sketch we model for you.

PLA · PETG · prototypes · name plates · gifts

Electronics repair

Diagnosis and repair for boards and devices, plus custom builds with ESP32 and Arduino when you need something that does not exist yet.

Fault finding · board repair · custom ESP32 / Arduino builds

Web development

Websites and business tools for local shops and institutions: service pages, catalogues, billing and management apps that work on a phone.

Websites · Firebase apps · billing and inventory tools · Marathi and English

About ColorCast

Made by people who build their own machines

ColorCast Creative Printing & Customization Studio started as a maker's workshop and grew into a full production studio.

Kedar Lohar founded the studio. The work spans hardware, software and design, so the same team that prints your shirt can also repair the controller board in your machine or build the app that bills your customers.

We prefer clear answers over big promises. If a job is not a good fit for our machines, we say so.
